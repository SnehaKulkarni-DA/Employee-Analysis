# Employee Stats Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📊 Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive view of workforce statistics, designed to support HR and business leaders in making data-driven decisions. It consolidates employee demographics, payroll distribution, and organizational trends into an interactive and visually engaging format.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Summary Metrics**
  
  - 1000 Total Employees
  - 113M Total Payroll
  - 0.09% Exit Rate
  - 44K Sum of Age

- **Interactive Filters**
  - Business Unit (Corporate, Manufacturing, R&D, Speciality Products)
  - Ethnicity (Asian, Black, Caucasian, etc.)
  - City (Austin, Beijing, Chicago, Miami, etc.)

- **Visual Insights**
  - **Payroll by Department**: IT, Engineering, Marketing, Sales, HR
  - **Gender Distribution**: Female vs Male (Donut chart)
  - **Raise % Gauge**: Track employee raise percentages
  - **Employees by Country**: United States, China, Brazil
  - **Employees by Year**: Growth trend from 1990–2020

## 🎯 Purpose
The dashboard is designed to:
- Provide HR teams with actionable insights into workforce composition.
- Highlight payroll allocation across departments.
- Track employee growth trends over time.
- Support diversity and inclusion analysis with demographic filters.
